- **Drop hint on posters (M17 W3, 2026-11-27):** the "Drop this picture on baphaelman.github.io/algorhythms to play it" line takes no room: it sits right-aligned beside the title or credit and hides when it doesn't fit or when anything lands on it. It shows on every bundled piece at 16:9; on square and A4 it hides for 7 of 13 bundled pieces (long titles). Options if it should show more: a smaller hint or a shorter text. Watch link: `#w&p=…`; once open, the address bar shows the editor link (W1). Leaving fullscreen with the player's own button keeps the watch view; Escape or the browser's exit lands in the Video workspace (W2).
- **Video bitrate (2026-11-23, M11):** the Quality presets set a bitrate *cap*; browsers spend far less on these frames and more bits barely improve them, so M11 gates decoded quality (≥ 40 dB PSNR) instead of "≥ 70 % of the target bitrate".
- **Video true peak (2026-11-23, M11):** a video's audio is limited to −1.5 dBTP (AAC) or −2 dBTP (Opus) so it stays ≤ −1 dBTP after decoding; the WAV keeps −1.
- **Camera key window (2026-11-23, M11.5):** a key's window is the seconds shown in a 16:9 frame of the same height; other aspects (9:16, the editor's wide view) scale it like Follow does.
- **Walk `over: 'chord'` (2026-11-23, M13 bug fix):** walks follow the union of sounding chords; a chord of two or more notes ends earlier chords on other parts too (a one-note chord only adds its note). The editor's Chords row still shows those as overlapping.
- **Rows (M10b, 2026-11-22).** A chord block with Notes: 1 sounds the chord's root (the row model voiced the middle tone). The Texture button adds an Arpeggio by default; Wander, Pulse and Hold are one click away.
- **Next offers only chord cards (M10a, 2026-11-22).** Its split, walk, thin, collapse and level suggestions go; note counts and textures return as chord-block and texture choices in M10b. Cards add seventh chords when four or more lines sound, triads otherwise.
- **Export look details (rev 13).** With nothing drawn ahead of the playhead, a following video puts the playhead 80 % across (35 % when the future shows), as an option ("Playhead at"). Posters default to no HUD (it would show end-of-piece values) but keep the title block. Still fixed, not options: type sizes and font, dot and ring sizes, the pitch-colour gradient.
- **Poster defaults (M9, PLAN §12 rev 12).** PNG or SVG of the whole piece, every line at full strength, no playhead or dots. 3840×2160 by default (square 2160² and A4 portrait 2480×3508 as options), current theme, optional transparent background, a title block with the name, length, key and a small AlgoRhythms credit (on by default), thickness on. SVG made in-house, no dependency.
- **Video export defaults (M8, PLAN §12 rev 11).** MP4 (H.264 + AAC; VP9/Opus fallback), 1920×1080 at 30 fps by default (9:16, 1:1, 720p and 60 fps as options), camera Follow (your current zoom) or Whole piece, current theme, HUD and thickness on. No new dependency: an in-house MP4 writer.
- **Live release and voices (M7).** Lifting a chord's keys within 60 ms of each other counts as one release, so Hold keeps the chord (and Fade or Return to tonic act on it); keys still down after 60 ms make it move to them, 60 ms after the first lift. Presses are never delayed. Where fewer than 4 voices are sounding, live mode adds voices from the pool (taking the nearest sounding voice's timbre and part) up to 4, so a chord is always playable; a recording writes them into the piece.
- **Live drawer and musical typing (M7-b).** Live is a third toggle in the view bar; opening it enters live mode and turns musical typing on. `A W S E D F T G Y H U J K` play from C4, `Z`/`X` change octave (C1–C7), `` ` `` or the transport's Typing button toggles it, and the button reads "Typing C4" while on. While typing is on, letters never trigger editor shortcuts; Space, Escape and ⌘/Ctrl shortcuts still work. Keys go by physical position (AZERTY gets the same layout). Live settings belong to you, not the piece (remembered in this browser, as is a connected MIDI device). The Live drawer can stay open beside Notes or Sound so Master edits are heard live. While live, the score dims and each live voice draws a trail up to the playhead; the piano ruler plays notes instead of auditioning.
- **Recording (M7-c).** A take starts at the playhead and replaces the piece from there on (one undo restores it). A chord pressed key by key within 15 ms records as one chord, and now also plays live as that chord from its first key. Releases record as you heard them: Hold writes nothing, Fade writes a level moment to 0, Return to tonic writes a unison on the tonic. Pitch bend and key velocity aren't recorded. An empty piece plays live with the whole voice pool.
- **Levels at splits and returns (v49).** A note that splits off starts at its parent's volume, including a fade in progress (from a separate session; only Tide Pools changes). A part coming back from silence starts at full volume, unless a level moment addresses it as it comes in (no piece changes).
- **Progression notes (v47).** The first chord sounded different because it was voiced close while the rest spread over the whole range; now every chord of a progression without a register is voiced close around the middle. Default spacing = glide + Next's hold (5.8 s per chord, or whole beats with a tempo); a typed chord without a length gets it too. Chord rows: name (click to change), length slider, ↑/↓, ×; the key's chord chips add a chord at the end.
- **Live instrument defaults (M7).** One key held plays a unison; several play exactly those notes (doubled across the lines); letting go holds the chord; glide 0.25 s, no stagger. All are settings in the Live drawer. Entering live mode stops playback (v1 records into silence only).
- **Chord name in tune-only stretches.** When every sounding line is carried by tunes (Little Round), the chord name above the score comes from the notes: each beat, each line's longest-held note counts, a name holds while its notes still fit and changes at most every 0.4 s. Whenever a chord-driven line sounds, the chord moments' names stand as before. On an empty piece in a major key, the unison suggestion reads "Start on C (unison)" next to the triad's "Start on C".
- **Progression (rev 10).** Palette key O, button `prog`; all palette buttons got 2 px narrower padding so 15 fit at 1280 px. A new one plays I vi IV V, one bar each (2 s each without a tempo). Type `IV 2, V 2, iii 2, vi 2`; the lane shows each chord's name. It has no dots on the score: Freeze turns it into separate chords to edit notes. A key change at exactly a chord's time follows the same rule as a hand-written chord: it counts only if the key moment comes first in the list.
- **Walk over chord (rev 10).** A walk with Walk over: Chord steps through the tones of the latest chord at or before each step, whichever part that chord targets. A chord that only says "current" or is muted doesn't count. The bass's leap is drawn as before, then moved to the nearest chord tone; if none fits the bass rules, it doesn't leap.
- **Several parts (rev 10).** The Part chooser is a row of chips (All plus each part) with an **All but these** switch. Deleting a part turns targets that named it into the exact notes they covered, so the sound doesn't change.
- **Tune rests, re-strike, Follow key (rev 10).** Type `r 1` for a rest. A rest fades the line out over 20 ms (or half the rest, if shorter), and the next note comes in over 10 ms and strikes. After a trailing rest, a chord or other moment brings the line back over at least 0.5 s, like a part coming in from silence. A repeated pitch re-strikes. The inspector has two checkboxes, **Follow key** (new tunes on) and **Re-strike repeats** (on). Follow key moves the tune by the smallest interval to the key at its start; a tritone goes down, the way chords carried by a key do.
- **Pulse custom order (rev 10).** Mode chooser: "Custom order" with an Order field (`1 3 2 4`, 1 = lowest line, values past the line count wrap). A rest doesn't advance the order; when lines join or leave, the order keeps its place. Switching to another mode drops the typed order (undo brings it back).
- **Speed gate measures the shipped code (v43).** The ≥ 10× real-time gate now runs the DSP bundled the way the worklet ships (Vite, minified), in Node, instead of through Vitest's module loader, which slowed every call. Same threshold. The DSP itself also got 1.2–1.3× faster with identical output. Heavy timbre pieces have less spare speed (about 13× here) than plain ones (about 31×).
- **Compact bar (v41).** Lines, Theme, HUD and Keep lines in order moved into a "View ▾" popover; help text into "?"; the bar spans the full width under the inspector too (the inspector is ~75 px shorter). The score keeps a minimum height (~204 px); the lanes shrink first, then an open drawer.
- **Master FX (v41).** Master is a tab beside Timbre in the Sound drawer; delay snaps to 1/16 … 1/2 including dotted values. Tone, reverb size and delay time changes now glide (15–50 ms), so live edits don't click.
- **Melody on (v41).** The whole line is highlighted on hover; the tune keeps its distance from the line when it moves.
- **Empty (v41).** Muted moments still count as moments for "first moment at 0:00". Right after New → Empty the Tonic menu has focus, so letter keys go to it until you click elsewhere.
- **Timbre moments (M6-b).** Default fade 2 s. Targets 'all', parts and listed notes include silent notes, so a note born later starts in the new timbre. Palette key B (buttons narrowed to fit 14 in one row at 1280 px). The Sound drawer gains Rename (updates notes and moments, one undo) and Delete (only when no note uses the timbre). A change mid-fade fades from what is sounding; the crossfade follows each pair's measured correlation, so the level moves smoothly (within 0.4 dB of a straight line). No re-strike on a change. Unison groups of mixed timbres that never change keep their old level (goldens unchanged).
- **Snap to beats (R9-9).** On by default whenever the piece has a tempo; turning it off is saved with the piece (`view.snapBeats`) and isn't undoable, like Loop. A dragged time goes to the nearer of the nearest beat and a note-change point within 8 px (ties go to the beat); a walk's sibling steps still win. Next appends at the first beat at or after the last end. Alt places freely everywhere, palette drops included. Arrow nudges, the ruler and Add at the playhead are unchanged. Land at the marker: the toggle shows the effective value (piece default or the moment's own) and stores `land` only when it differs; on a landing bar the end handle sits at the bar's start, and dragging it left lengthens the glide while the end stays on the marker.
- **Tune moment rules (R9-4).** A tune plays on the lowest line of its target. It can't start sound from silence: it needs a sounding line. When a tune lands on a line of its own part, the two merge and the tune carries on along the merged line. Walks don't step the tune's line while it plays. Melody notes don't change the HUD chord. A repeated pitch is held, not struck again. Each glide defaults to 0.1 s, cut short when a note is shorter; with land on, each note arrives on its start.
- **Crossfade window.** A merge or split crossfades over the last third of its glide, at least 0.1 s, keeping summed power constant (within 0.01 dB), and the drawn strands follow the same curve. Merge moments and count deaths crossfade too, so a merge's Fade setting now only matters when no line survives. The inspector will show Fade only then (queued with R9-8).
- **Pulse and tune editing (R9-7).** Palette keys: R for pulse, E for tune. A new pulse plays eighths for two bars with accents 1, .5, .75, .5. A beat counts as a quarter note. On the lane, a pulse bar shows its accent cycle and a tune bar shows its notes as dashes; a tune's end handle sets its last note's length. Palette buttons are 4 px narrower, so all 13 fit one row at 1280 px. Walk region-end drags now snap to beats too, and the Time field moves a walk's or pulse's whole region.
- **Tune dots and typed notes (R9-8).** Dragging a dot sideways keeps the note's end in place, so the previous note's length changes too; moving the first note moves the tune's start. Dots are tab stops: ↑/↓ moves a scale step (Alt for a semitone), ←/→ moves by a beat or 0.1 s. The "Type" field takes note names only (no MIDI numbers); a note with no length repeats the previous length; notes are shown spelled in the key, so `Bb4` in C major shows as `A#4`. Merge's Fade field is relabelled "Fade (when no line survives)", because the compiled score doesn't say whether a merge has a survivor.
- **Pulse sound and MIDI (R9-3b).** Each hit rises over a short attack (12 ms on crisp timbres such as pluck, up to 50 ms on soft ones), peaks at its accent, decays to the floor, and re-strikes the timbre's articulation; hits never share a bloom. MIDI CC11 now carries pulse, crossfade fades and weight, written when the value moves 2 steps and at most every 5 ms. With CC11 on, a note strikes at the velocity of its line's peak weight, because velocity can't follow a merge. The export option is relabelled "CC11 expression (pulse, fades, level)".
- **Share links (M5, done).** New links start `#p=2.`: defaults are stripped, numbers rounded (times to 1 ms; tune lengths, pulse steps and walk grids to 1 µs, because 1 ms drifted 57 ms over Night Ferry) and a deflate dictionary is used. Old links still open. Every example now fits in about 1,000–1,100 characters, and the rewritten compositions in 1,300–1,700. The old compositions currently on the site are still 5,500–19,800 characters until the rewrite ships. Share now opens a "Share link…" dialog with Copy and, over 2,000 characters, Save as file.
- **Selection, copy and paste, Freeze, piano ruler (M5).** A marquee replaces the selection; ⌘/Ctrl+Shift adds to it. ⌘/Ctrl-click toggles one moment, and ⌘/Ctrl+A selects all moments except the piece key. Copy, cut and paste work on moments, pasting at the playhead; clipboard text that isn't moments is ignored. The Freeze button covers chord, split and walk; a frozen split can only be undone, since Unfreeze would need a new core field. Piano ruler: on a selected chord, clicking a key toggles that note; on a pin, it moves the pin; otherwise it plays the pitch while the transport is stopped.
- **Touch editing (M5, §11.3b).** Tap a palette item to add it at the playhead. Tap selects, and a drag past 10 px moves. Double-tap a line to add a note point; tapping one shows its voice, note and Hz. Pinch zooms and two fingers pan. A long press opens a menu with Duplicate, Copy, Delete and Paste, plus three sticky toggles in place of Alt and Shift: Snap, "Ends set stagger" and Select more (which enables the marquee). Each shows a chip while it's on. Dots get 44 px touch targets.
- **FM, noise and stretched partials (M6-a).** FM uses its own modulator phase, and its index is capped near Nyquist to stop aliasing (top-of-range aliasing −53 to −66 dB). Noise is seeded white noise through a band-pass that follows each note's pitch. Stretched timbres use up to 16 partials at n^(1+stretch). The new bell preset combines stretch and FM and is quieter by design (RMS −28 dBFS on Tidewater). Existing presets and pieces render bit-identically.
- **Timbre editor and presets (M6-c).** The Sound drawer has its own button beside Notes. Editing a built-in timbre stores a copy in the piece under the same name, with Revert and Duplicate. Preset files are `*.algorhythms-timbre.json`, and a loaded preset applies by default to the notes using the timbre being edited. Articulation's Strike setting is Auto, Crisp or Soft. New presets: epiano, flute, marimba and brass, each within 2 dB of the existing presets' level. Master FX in the drawer is not built yet.
- **Dense lanes v2 (replaces the compact rows you rejected).**
  - Rows stay full height, and the lane area scrolls inside itself with fades at the edges. At 1024×768 Kite Day's rows take 232 px and the lane area shows 113 px of them.
  - Level moments sit on the Level row: each one is a marker on the envelope, with no bar. When several levels share a time they stack in up to four sub-rows. A level's ramp length is edited in the inspector only.
  - Pins get their own thin Pins row, which appears only when the piece has pins. Pin and level labels show on hover, focus or selection.
  - A divider between the score and the lanes can be dragged, or moved with ↑/↓ and Home/End. Double-click or Enter resets it. The score keeps at least 25 % of the height and the lanes at least 44 px. The split is remembered per viewer, in this browser only.
- **Land at the marker (R9-2, engine; the toggle comes with R9-9).**
  - A land moment starts its glide length + stagger before its marker, so its notes arrive on the marker. Near 0:00 the glide shortens to fit instead of arriving late.
  - It reads the key at its marker. If nothing is sounding when its lead-in starts, it waits and acts at the marker.
  - A later moment whose marker falls inside the lead-in interrupts it.
  - Splits and merges now use the moment's stagger and order like other moments, which you approved in rev 9. The five original examples change timing slightly; the compositions are unchanged.
- **Parts in the editor (R9-6).**
  - The Notes drawer shows one group per part. Each header has a name field, delete, and whole-part timbre, pan, gain and range. Notes without a part sit under "Unassigned", and each note row has a Part menu.
  - A piece without parts gains one "New part" button. With no parts it puts every note in "Part 1"; with parts it groups the unassigned notes into "Part n". A note's own "New part…" puts just that note in a new part.
  - When a part's notes differ, its controls say "Mixed": timbre shows a disabled Mixed choice, pan and gain read Mixed and a drag sets every note, and a range field left blank leaves that bound alone.
  - Two parts can't share a name. Deleting a part unassigns its notes; moments that targeted it ask inline, then target those same notes directly.
  - The inspector's Part chooser sits after Time. A moment that targets a part says so in its header and lane tooltip. New notes from growing the pool start unassigned.

- **The start is a moment (M4.5-27).**
  - A new piece opens on one chord at 0:00 with a single note (A3). Deleting it leaves silence.
  - From silence, only chord, unison and key are enabled in the palette. Shift, split, merge, spread, walk, note and pin say "no note is sounding; start with a chord or a unison".
  - From silence, suggestions offer "Start on Am" (the tonic triad) and "Start on A" (a unison). You didn't ask for this.
  - A chord that starts from silence with no register is voiced close, in a window of 12 + 3 × (number of notes) semitones around the middle of the range. Spread over the whole range, a triad lost its third.
  - Old files and share links open and sound the same, and saving writes the new format. The Notes drawer's "At start" field is gone.
  - The opening is a point on the lane, as wide as its label, so it no longer pushes a later moment onto a second row (fixed in the M4.5 final pass).

- **Free octaves stay inside the chord's register (follow-up to 29a).** When a chord targets only some lines and the register runs out of free notes, the extra notes no longer drop below the register or cross the lines it doesn't touch. They take free octaves inside the register first, then between the untouched neighbours within the lines' range, and merge only when neither has room. The Cartographers' Rain chord `CrChd2` had put two notes at 59 and 43, below a register of 60–84 and under eight untouched lines. "Between the untouched neighbours" means the room stops at the nearest untouched line on that side, including untouched lines inside the register below or above the targeted ones. In Cartographers' Rain, two of `CrChd2`'s notes now merge on 79. The piece is 0.13 s longer, and its warning is gone.

- **Key carry-forward (M4.5-36).**
  - "Carry it forward" is read as: a key change offers to move the fixed notes up to the next key change, and later key moments are not shifted.
  - Fixed notes are remapped by scale degree. The tonic moves the shorter way, with ties going down.
  - The prompt reads like this: "9 moments until the end use fixed notes. Move them to E lydian?". Move joins the key edit's undo entry.
  - A named chord stays a named chord when only the tonic changes, for example Dmaj7#11 becomes Amaj7#11. When the scale changes, it becomes the list of pitches it plays.
  - "Home to …" suggestions now follow the key. "Thin to …" and "Collapse to …" a non-tonic note still write fixed pitches, because the format can't say them relative to the key yet, so they get the prompt like any other fixed notes.
  - Follow-up: a note change whose starting note is held from before the key change is no longer remapped there, because remapping it made it miss its note.
- **Weights (M4.5-29b).**

  - MIDI velocity is √weight, scaled down evenly when the loudest note would pass 127.
  - Changing a weight doesn't freeze the chord.
  - The wobble effect is gone. The "wobble" preset stays, without the beating.
