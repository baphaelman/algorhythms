## Gaps and findings (read first)

1. **A piece without a camera defaults to Auto in the dialog.** Resolved (integrator, 2026-11-23): the owner's "new videos default to Auto" wins; the acceptance line now reads "exported with Follow, draws exactly as before", which passes. The editor toggle also shows Auto for such a piece.
2. **The editor toggle on a piece without a camera shows Auto**, not Follow (C3: Auto or none → Auto's keys). It's off by default, so the editor is unchanged until it's switched on.
3. **Auto is wider than the hand cameras where they crop on purpose:** Murmuration's low drone, Calling Changes' paper-coloured shaker (e.g. at 1 s, 66 st against the hand camera's 40, and 84 against 56). Auto frames every drawn line and doesn't read colours.
4. **Widest window fixed at 14 s**, not tempo-aware (Crease's hand camera opens to 18 s).
5. **Auto's key times sit on a 1 s grid** from the first note, not on bars or beats.
6. **No lean-in at two-line crossings:** convergences need ≥ 3 lines. Auto also ends its pull-back on the last frame, while the hand cameras finish early and hold.
7. **No touch pinch** on the preview (trackpad pinch and wheel work); **no ease picker** per key (keys can carry an ease in the file and links, not set in the UI).
8. The editor shows Auto at 16:9 whole-piece keys, scaled to its canvas: not the video's own section or aspect if those differ.
9. Keys aren't checked against the piece's length: a key past the end is kept and holds.

## Interactions pass (Camera controls, editor toggle)

Chromium-dev, Kite Day, Keys selected; screenshots at 1280×720, 1440×900, 1920×1080 and 600×800, light and dark.
- **Layout:** markers sit under the slider within its width, in time order, ≥ 10 px, with a visible border in both themes; + Key, Delete and the count share one row; no sideways scroll; the view bar keeps the Camera toggle on one row at every width (new `m11-5-acceptance.spec`, 8 cases).
- **Keyboard:** Tab goes slider → each marker in time order → + Key → Delete (Delete only with a key selected); ←/→ move the key and the preview follows ("Key 3 of 6", preview at 22 s); a focused marker shows a ring; Escape on a marker closes the dialog with focus back on Menu; the toggle works with Enter (Space plays, by design, §11.5). New `m11-5-acceptance.spec` "Tab: the preview slider, then each key…".
- **Text:** one hint line per mode ("Your keys. Select one, then drag or scroll the preview."); the marker help is screen-reader only.
- **Issue found and fixed:** keys outside a custom Section (above).
