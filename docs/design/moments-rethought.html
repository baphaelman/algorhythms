Moments, rethought

The editor has fifteen kinds of moment, seventeen with the planned mirror and arpeggio. They do seven different jobs. Some are instants, some are regions, some change one line, and four of them set the same thing. Below: three ways to clean that up, a playable sketch of the one I recommend, and a Next that picks chords the way a musician would.

My pick · option B

Every moment becomes a block on one of five rows: Key, Chords, Texture, Melody, and Level and sound. A block lasts until the next block on its row. The chords become a real progression, which is what lets Next reason about them. The smarter Next does not need the overhaul: it can ship first, in today's editor.

What's wrong today

Four moments set one thing

chord, unison, split and merge all decide which notes sound and how many, and chord already has Notes: n. shift, spread and the planned mirror all reshape the notes already sounding.

"Moment" means four things

An instant (chord), a region (walk, pulse, tune, progression), a setting (key), or one line (note, pin, tune). The lane shows them all the same way, so a pulse arpeggio and a key change look alike.

Next doesn't know music yet

It draws related chords at random from a bag of root moves (up a fourth or fifth, the relative chord, a neighbour) and looks only at the chord now sounding. It knows nothing of phrases, cadences or the progression so far. Add → Chord always gives the home chord, or IV when you're already on it.

How the smarter Next decides

In plain words

Chords have jobs. Home feels at rest. Near home is calm, in another colour. Lift moves away. Tension pulls back toward home. Surprise borrows a chord from outside the key.

Most music cycles home, away, tension, home, in phrases of four and eight chords. The fourth chord often ends on tension, like a question, and the eighth goes home, like the answer.

Next shows one card per job, marks the best fit, and says in one sentence what each card would do.

For a musician
  • A transition table from common practice and pop: V→I, IV→V, ii→V, vi→IV; deceptive V→vi; plagal IV→I; borrowed iv, ♭VI, ♭VII; secondary dominants V/V, V/vi, V/IV. In minor: i–iv–V, i–♭VI–♭VII, raised V, Dorian IV.
  • Phrase position: half cadence at chord 4, set-up at 7, authentic cadence at 8, tonic at phrase starts.
  • Loop completion: when the last chords match a known progression (I–V–vi–IV, I–vi–IV–V, vi–IV–I–V, vi–ii–V–I, IV–V–iii–vi, Pachelbel, i–♭VII–♭VI–V, i–♭VI–♭III–♭VII, i–iv–♭VII–♭III), its next chord is boosted, more for longer matches.
  • Voice leading: +12% per tone shared with the chord now sounding. No immediate repeats; A–B–A back-steps are damped.
  • Numerals use the app's major-scale ruler (♭VI in minor), as you ruled on 21 Nov.
Elsewhere in the editor
  • Add → Chord, and its hover preview, gives the best fit between the chord before the playhead and the chord after it. Today it always gives I, or IV when you're on I.
  • The chord picker marks the best fits for that spot (★ best, good), from the chords on both sides.
  • New melodies put chord tones on strong beats, step between them, arch over the phrase, and end phrases on a stable note. They re-fit when the chords change.
  • Arpeggios restart on each bar, so the bass lands on beat 1.
  • Wander steps over chord tones by default, and the lowest line takes the chord's root unless you pick another bass.

What I'd build for option B, in order

  1. Theory core: chord jobs, the transition table, loops, phrase position, voice-leading score, with tests. It ships first, in today's editor: Next, the Add → Chord default and the picker marks. About 2–3 tasks.
  2. File format v3: rows and blocks, compiled into today's moments. Old pieces convert on open and sound the same; the rare moment that fits no row (a shift aimed at one part, a pin) stays as a hand edit on its line. About 2–3 tasks.
  3. The lane: the five rows, drag and resize blocks, the + gaps. About 2 tasks.
  4. Inspectors per row, and a palette cut down to Chord, Texture, Melody, Level, Sound and Key. About 2 tasks.
  5. Textures that read the Chords row: Hold, Wander, Arpeggio, Pulse; Drift later. 1–2 tasks.
  6. Melody defaults that re-fit to the chords. 1 task.
  7. Examples and compositions re-saved in v3, plus a "Like the video" template in New. 1 task.
