# M15 acceptance audit (2026-11-25, base 243d63d)

Scope: PLAN §15 "M15. Play the poster": *Accept when*, the Integrator calls, Storage, V1's calls; build-log entries "M15 V1 contract", "M15 V1: the renderer", "M15 V2: the workspace's Video mode" (with its dialog checklist), "M15 V2 handoff", "M15 V2 (resume)".

**Changed in this audit (V3):**
- **Keys on the timeline span the section** (the brief's open item). V2 had put them on the whole piece's axis, keys outside the section included. The Keys row now runs from the section's in to its out: keys outside it aren't on it, a key can't be dragged or arrowed out of it, as the old dialog's markers under its slider. m11-5's criterion "with a section, only its keys are on the timeline" is restored (plus: a marker sits at its time's share of the lane). `KeyMarkers`' `keep` prop is gone.
- **Over the poster, Auto showed as Follow but hid the Window and disabled Playhead at.** Every piece without a stored camera is Auto, so the Window ("still sets the seconds", integrator call) was unreachable. Over the poster the panel now treats Auto as Follow (the export already did).
- **Travels listed only explicit text boxes.** A poster with the default title and credit (no `texts` in the doc) had no text switches, so the title couldn't travel (PLAN: "The title can be switched"). The list now shows the doc's texts or the defaults, named by kind when the text is a template ("Title", "Credit"); switching one writes the texts into the doc.
- **Owner's notes on the Video workspace (2026-11-25):**
  1. *The end card's zoom out.* Over the poster the ending was a 2 s crossfade from the last row to the poster, with no zoom. And playing the preview stopped at the music's end: the player stops itself at the piece's end, and the preview read that as Pause before switching to the end card's silent clock. Now the ending zooms (`drawPosterEndZoom`: centre linear, height geometric, from the last frame to Whole, with the finished poster and its credit fading in). It lands exactly on the end card, the same scrubbed, played (twice), after a Window change, and in the export's frames (`drawVideoMoment`, shared by preview and worker). The credit fades in only once the band below the poster has room.
  2. *Section times cut off.* The start and end read as text sized to the time (`--len` ch, content-box). A click or Tab edits one (selected on focus); Enter or blur commits, Escape cancels.
  3. *Getting around.* The preview's time slider is gone. A press or drag on the timeline's strip seeks; its playhead is a keyboard slider ("Preview time": arrows 1 s, Shift 10 s, Alt 0.1 s, Home/End the video's start and end). Play sits under the preview on the right. The section handles keep their drag and keys; the Keys row is unchanged. Specs moved off the slider via `seekPreview` (hook `previewSeek`, exact) or the strip.
- **CI fix (0daa646):** `poster-edit.spec` "a recoloured layer shows in the video preview" used the old dialog's selector; it now reads the Video workspace's preview (at 40 s over the poster) and checks that undo removes the colour from both. No other spec has old-dialog selectors (the remaining `export-close` is the audio dialog's).
- New checks: e2e `m15-acceptance.spec` (12 tests); unit `render/poster-video.test` "a moved layer lights up in its own place…", "the ending zooms out from the last frame to the end card, the same every time".

**Runs on this tree:** see the end.

Status key: **P** passed locally on chromium-dev, firefox-dev and webkit-dev; **P (C+F)** passed locally on Chromium and Firefox, with WebKit left to CI; **U** a unit test (`pnpm test`, engine-independent pure code).
