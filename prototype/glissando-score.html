Glissando Score

Twelve voices. Each line is one oscillator's pitch over time, and the sound and the picture are drawn from the same numbers.

Lines

Space plays and pauses. Pinch, Ctrl/⌘-scroll or − = changes the window.

The score

Each voice is a list of moves: hold a note, then glide to the next with a smootherstep ease. When the chord changes, voices are sorted by pitch and matched to the sorted chord tones, so lines rarely cross. A split is a new voice born at its parent's pitch that glides away.

The sound

Pitch is stored as a MIDI note number n and turned into hertz with f(n) = 440·2^((n−69)/12). Each curve is sampled 100 times a second and handed to a Web Audio oscillator as one frequency automation, with a matching gain curve. Gliding in n rather than hertz makes every interval move at an even speed to the ear.

The picture

The same arrays are drawn with time on x and note number on y, so vertical gaps are musical intervals. The camera follows the playhead, rings mark voices that are moving, and at the end it pulls back to show the whole score in one frame.
